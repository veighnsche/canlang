The completed source keeps confirmed `from`/`until` separate from actual `seated`/`cleared` and `estimated_until`. Reserve confirms or moves a reservation; a confirmed change requires a reason. Guest edits use canonical Booking CRUD and retain invariant checks. Seating a confirmed reservation retains its selected table and planned interval; relocation goes through reserve first. Actual occupants remain until clear, independently of estimates. Owner-local invariants reject insufficient capacities, disabled live tables, overlapping planned reservations and physical double occupancy. Cleared history does not freeze later table capacity. Due/overdue/estimate-overlap conflicts expose the actual affected rows using the bounded text/details/table primitives and explicit bound forms. Café creation and renaming use canonical CRUD, preserving its original location after creation.
