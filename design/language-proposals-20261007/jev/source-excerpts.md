4: 
5: canlang is an AI-native, Cucumber-style Given / When / Then DSL built specifically for SaaS and running on workerd.

10: 
11: canlang targets companies that want to replace expensive SaaS subscriptions with custom, AI-generated applications tailored to their own operations. Design decisions should make those applications economical to generate, run, and change as the company's needs evolve.
12: 
13: The product bet is that many company SaaS needs can be met by tailored applications built from records, permissions, business rules, workflows, and standard interfaces. Making those applications economical to generate and maintain is the focus of the language.
