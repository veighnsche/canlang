# Methods, authority and evidence strength

This evaluates frozen product DESIGN at its declared draft stage. Compiler, standard library and infrastructure implementation are outside the scored defects. Desired `.mjs` is handwritten, never treated as generated output. A proposed contract is design evidence; syntax acceptance is not semantic/type/import correctness, rendering, BDD execution or translation fidelity.

| Evidence kind | Available evidence | Permitted inference | Prohibited inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen byte inventory | Root SHA256 verification: all 115 hashes match, 0 mismatches; manifest 3d853e5a13a3c3eac7f150dfa742e26e0d9bf6c652b20fb05da4aa0b2b576106 | Common immutable source/version and artifact counts | Correctness/completeness |
| Requirements/design/manual review | Baseline paths/lines; B rules and behavior traces; C interface/target sources | Expressed rules, contradictions, omissions, responsibility and scope | Existing executable app or guaranteed prospective behavior |
| Tokenizer/scripts | Pinned tokenizer input bytes, artifact/category counts and repeat occurrences from root support | Model-source token count and visible context/byte arithmetic | Hidden model reasoning/billing, independent semantic success, universal compression |
| Bounded AI draft attempts | Pinned protocol, saved artifacts and matched repair policy; settings/tooling retained | Observed attempt-specific generation/edit/repair burden and omissions | Statistical reliability, production deployment or broad DSL superiority |
| Economic arithmetic | Hypothetical workloads/support/rates/horizon and primary provider tariffs from C | Break-even sensitivity under those assumptions | Actual bill, engineering cost, demand or realized savings |
| JEV mock-company classification | Three wholly reworded equivalent requests, exact responses/probabilities | Simulated advice given supplied facts/assumptions | Customer testimony, market demand or willingness to pay |
| Later execution/user research | Not available in this audit | Clearly identified future validation need | Failed production test merely because implementation is not begun |

Fair comparisons pin company outcomes/users/access/data/support/horizon before attempts. Count complete authored app, requirements/docs/interaction context and per-app glue for both approaches; libraries/services are reusable on both. Conventional framework maturity/pretraining/tool availability differs from unfamiliar draft Can and is disclosed, not hidden. No statistical sample or real usability interview exists. Counterexamples show scope, not universal failure. One suite replacement counts only actually avoidable charges after all required composed workflows, retained seats/contracts and migration pass. At present workflows/economics remain conditional.
