## Narrow example-language limitation

**Category: missing contract, L3C.** Frozen DESIGN:380 supplies only `self` and `other` in the current team, plus `outsider` in another team; `as=finance` still selects self. A representative successful finance case needs claimant, first reviewer and finance approver as three distinct current members. No defined declaration provisions that third same-team account. Both subjects identify this rather than inventing syntax (single `:89`; packages `:102–103`). Their boundary/denial examples remain useful, but do not establish a successful second approval or a finance-rejection→correction→reimbursement journey.

Using outsider as claimant or first reviewer could make the local equality guards pass in a hand-seeded historical state. That does not demonstrate the specified three-current-member journey or its earlier authorized transitions; it must not be reported as equivalent coverage. Likewise a fixture can represent a historical FinanceDecision without exercising its authorized creation. No claim is made that every conceivable positive operation row is syntactically impossible.

Smallest improvement: define test-only provisioning/binding of additional same-team accounts and their memberships/roles within the existing fixture/example contract. Benefit: express the actual separation of duties without fake outsiders or production scaffolding. Tradeoff: account/role setup and caller selection need explicit deterministic semantics. Re-evaluate with successful finance approval, finance rejection followed by correction preserving both old decision children, exact threshold, distinct actors, stale version and reimbursement gating; setup must be valid before invoking each operation. This is a proposed contract clarification/capability, not new syntax implemented here.
