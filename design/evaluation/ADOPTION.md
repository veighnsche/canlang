# Adoption and AI efficiency evaluation — owner A

**Product-design conclusion:** Can has a credible, bounded value proposition for company-owned record/approval/workflow applications when common authentication, storage, browser/MCP, file and replay contracts are supplied once. The independent small-app attempts support expressibility and concise Can authoring; they do **not** establish equivalent-behavior token superiority, statistical reliability or total ownership savings. Narrow app/target corrections and test/installation/intake clarifications should come before broader language extensions. Company P is a plausible early scope; Company W has prospective value only after all its required composed workflows and historical/recovery obligations pass. Company S's required offline/routing suite remains outside full replacement. Ownership economics depend chiefly on real avoidable charges and stewardship, not cheap hosting or app count.

## Integrated value, uncertainty and advice

At an assumed **$450/month truly avoidable P bill**, the 24-month scenarios yield **+$8,650 low support /−$1,040 high support**. At **$900/month W**, they yield **+$10,980 /−$21,500**. These are reproducible hypotheses including future incumbent configuration/support, setup/migration/training/overlap, staff, steward, nonzero shared-platform funding and incremental providers. Retained services cancel only when genuinely equal; historical archive, commitments/seats or extra file/recovery requirements can change the result. Platform development is neither free nor rebuilt and charged anew per company. C's current primary tariff subtotal—$14.45 W base + mail before other use/platform/support—is a cost input, not a total-saving proof. Actual costs, real company willingness to review/own and complete-suite acceptance remain unknown.

Three fully rewritten **JEV mock-company** requests returned `jev-1.13.0`; full probabilities/confidence/raw requests are in [evidence](evidence/adoption/jev/README.md). All selected bounded future validation: P probability .96/.96/.97, W .91/.48/.93, S .71/.70/.75. W second request has confidence .23 and retain probability .42, so selected-label unanimity conceals substantial uncertainty. Source/criterion equivalence was reread; no factual reversal found, no rationale supplied. Pilot cost is nonzero/unquoted and may not be worthwhile; obtain a real scope/quote/cap before spending. S pilot means isolated inspections retaining route/offline software, never full-suite savings. These classifications are simulated advice, not customer testimony, demand, willingness to pay, approval threshold or a command to pilot. Actual buyers can reasonably retain/configure incumbents. B optional-composition/history consultations and C reviewer-identity consultations disagree; source causes and limited evidence, rather than votes, determine the correction sequence.
