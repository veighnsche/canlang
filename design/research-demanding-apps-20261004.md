# More demanding company-app drafts

Research date: 2026-10-04. This is a candidate design portfolio, not approval to implement it or a claim of market popularity. Official vendor documentation establishes concrete workflow slices; the proposed company outcomes, scope boundaries and Can implications below are research inferences. No compiler, library or runtime implementation, JEV consultation or external business action was performed.

## Selection and existing coverage

The useful target is a company replacing a particular paid workflow with several small apps it owns, sharing business identities and canonical operations. Compare the same outcome and workload rather than an entire vendor suite against one Can app. Current contracts can change while retaining app/package identity; missing semantics should be exposed, not concealed behind imaginary helpers.

The [draft inventory](../draft/README.md) has 39 requirement/source pairs. Inspection of requirements found existing customer support in [CanDesk](../draft/CanDesk.md), sales records in [CanCRM](../draft/CanCRM.md), purchasing/order/receipt ownership in [CanPurchase](../draft/CanPurchase.md), private applications and budget review in [CanGrant](../draft/CanGrant.md), staff training in [CanLearn](../draft/CanLearn.md), finalized meeting evidence in [CanBoard](../draft/CanBoard.md), and source-backed aggregates in [CanReport](../draft/CanReport.md). CanTable is restaurant seating, not an Airtable clone. [CanMail](../draft/CanMail.md) handles physical mail and parcels, not an email inbox. Many current workflows already exceed CRUD: asynchronous provider outcomes, frozen revisions, compensation and privacy exist. The candidates add new pressures—untrusted AI output, evidence retrieval, multistep tools, media derivation and recurring discovery—rather than assuming existing apps are mere CRUD.

## Candidates

Each candidate states a bounded replacement slice. Names are working identities for future drafts, not implemented declarations.

### 1. CanInbox — departmental email sorter (new; first priority)

**Outcome:** a small service company gives purchasing, sales and support messages to the correct staff without everyone scanning every inbox. Receive authorized mailbox events, deduplicate messages, retain threads/attachments, apply deterministic rules followed by optional AI classification, propose routing and distinguish private from shared scope. Review uncertain cases, permit correction, snooze to a dated follow-up and record explainable routing history. **Primitives:** message/thread data, mailbox delegation, team/queue grants, inbound events, schedules, AI structured classifications, bounded file ingestion. **Authority/recovery:** message text cannot grant access or choose privileged recipients; changed routing requires current grants. Duplicate events do not create tickets; failed synchronization exposes a cursor gap; provider acceptance differs from delivered reply. Compose with CanDesk/CRM using their owning operations. **Slice:** Front company rules/shared inbox assignment, not all channels or mailbox hosting. [Front rules](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2032) document centralized automation and private/shared restrictions.

### 2. CanDiscover — repeated opportunity discovery (new; first priority)

**Outcome:** a specialist supplier receives a reviewable weekly shortlist of tenders, partnership openings or relevant funding calls. Run a bounded search plan over declared sources; retain query/date/cursor, source URL, retrieved evidence and deadline timezone. Extract candidate claims with citations, deduplicate both canonical URL and opportunity identity, distinguish changed evidence from new opportunity, rank against company criteria and queue review. Only an authorized reviewer promotes an opportunity to CRM/proposal work. **Primitives:** scheduled bounded pagination, source retrieval, AI extraction/scoring, provenance, deadlines, semantic duplicate proposals and shared identities. **Recovery:** incomplete source coverage is partial, never “no opportunities”; source failures resume from checkpoints; prompt injection cannot change search budget or trigger outreach; expired items remain historical. **Slice:** a company-specific Feedly collection/triage and AlphaSense monitoring workflow using available sources, excluding licensed-content parity. [Feedly deduplication](https://docs.feedly.com/article/218-how-does-deduplication-work) and [AlphaSense alerts](https://help.alpha-sense.com/hc/en-us/articles/41815509396371-Maximizing-Your-Monitoring-Tools-in-AlphaSense) support this slice.

### 3. CanChat — private work conversations with optional AI (new; first priority)

**Outcome:** an operations team discusses cases and asks a bounded assistant without copying sensitive context between tools. Private conversations and explicitly shared rooms retain ordered messages, attachments and model runs. An AI reply references authorized context; a proposed business action previews exact arguments and awaits approval where required. **Primitives:** ordered message history, membership grants, incremental response status or polling, cancelable model calls, file inputs, canonical operation capabilities and cost budgets. **Recovery:** canceled or failed generation remains distinguishable from a completed answer; reconnect does not duplicate messages; revoked membership blocks subsequent retrieval and tools; model text never impersonates human approval. Keep collaborative document editing outside scope. **Slice:** Open WebUI private/team chat, file inputs and scoped tools plus Dify conversational workflows, not their whole AI platform. [Open WebUI permissions](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/authentication-access/rbac/permissions/), [chat sharing](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/chat-conversations/chat-features/chatshare/) and [Dify workflows](https://dify.ai/workflows) describe relevant boundaries.

### 4. CanGallery — company image collection and approval (new; first priority)

**Outcome:** a design agency or retailer locates approved images and supplies clients with the right version. Upload batches, finalize originals, create bounded thumbnails, tag manually or with AI, propose duplicate groups, collect reviewer approval and publish selected assets to an explicitly scoped collection. Preserve creator/client/license and permitted-use evidence with expiry. **Primitives:** image metadata, file ownership, asynchronous derivatives, galleries/preview, moderation proposals, collection grants and expiring delivery references. **Recovery:** derivative failure does not corrupt the original; pending moderation is not approval; deleted/revoked assets leave no newly authorized public delivery; client sharing does not expose other clients’ assets. No pixel editor or video timeline. **Slice:** Cloudinary Media Library asset discovery and moderation queues, not its full CDN/transformation service. [Media Library](https://cloudinary.com/documentation/media_library_for_developers), [moderation](https://cloudinary.com/documentation/moderate_assets).

### 5. CanKnowledge — evidence-backed policy assistant (new)

**Outcome:** staff get answers grounded in their current approved procedures. Ingest/version documents, extract searchable passages, review publication and answer with precise source citations and effective dates. Distinguish superseded guidance, contradictory documents and unavailable evidence. **Primitives:** document extraction/indexing, resource-level retrieval grants, AI grounded generation, document versions and publication schedules. **Recovery:** ingestion failure leaves the old approved revision usable but labeled; revocation removes retrieval access; unsupported answers abstain or route to an owner; cited evidence remains inspectable under current grants. Compose with CanLearn for training assignments without treating an AI answer as certification. **Slice:** Dify retrieval/chatflow and Open WebUI knowledge chat, excluding generic model administration. [Dify workflow nodes](https://dify.ai/workflows), [Open WebUI resource groups](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/authentication-access/rbac/groups/).

### 6. Document-to-purchase pack (enrich CanPurchase/CanExpense; first priority)

**Outcome:** a finance clerk checks emailed invoices against approved orders and physical receipts before recording approved payable evidence. Extract supplier/line/currency/tax claims from finalized documents; show page evidence; compare to canonical order/receipts; flag duplicate invoices and mismatches; a human corrects extraction and approves the exact reviewed revision. **Primitives:** PDF/OCR input, structured uncertain AI results, exact money/units, owning supplier identities, matching queries, separation-of-duties and durable approval. **Recovery:** illegible pages or absent receipts block an exact match; repeated import does not double liability; new document versions invalidate old approval; a supplier document cannot replace verified bank details. **Slice:** Airtable AI document extraction composed with Zip intake/procurement review, not a general accounting suite. [Airtable extraction](https://support.airtable.com/articles/8052242094-using-airtable-ai-in-fields), [Zip intake-to-procure](https://zip.com/products/intake-to-procure).

### 7. CanEnrich — reviewed prospect enrichment (new; compose with CanCRM)

**Outcome:** sales staff fill missing company/contact facts with evidence while respecting spend and outreach exclusions. Try configured providers in an explicit waterfall, retain provider/time/confidence, compare conflicts and request review before updating canonical CRM identities. **Primitives:** typed external calls, exact provider-credit budgets, field provenance, staged updates, dedupe/alias proposals, consent/suppression data. **Recovery:** rate limits resume without repeating paid successes; unknown provider outcomes reconcile; invalid/conflicting identities remain candidates; enrichment cannot activate outreach or resurrect suppressed contacts. **Slice:** Clay enrichment waterfalls, not its proprietary data supply. [Clay waterfalls](https://university.clay.com/docs/building-a-data-waterfall) document ordered providers and conditional runs.

### 8. CanOutreach — approval-led follow-up sequences (new; compose with CRM/Inbox)

**Outcome:** a salesperson sends a small approved sequence to verified consenting prospects and stops on reply or opt-out. Freeze content and recipients at approval, schedule in local business windows, check current suppression and ownership before each send, then track reply/bounce rather than equating send acceptance with engagement. **Primitives:** calendars, canonical contacts, email provider events, delivery identities, approval/version binding and suppression. **Recovery:** duplicate enrollment is blocked; an uncertain send is reconciled before retry; changed consent cancels pending work; AI drafts cannot enroll recipients. **Slice:** Apollo sequence enrollment/pause/resume with duplicate and permission checks, not its contact database. [Apollo enrollment](https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/4409396985741-Add-Contacts-to-a-Sequence).

### 9. Support triage and SLA pack (enrich CanDesk)

**Outcome:** support staff meet company-specific response commitments and receive useful AI summaries. Classify intent, suggest owning queue, summarize with linked messages, calculate business-hours deadlines, escalate unclaimed work and hand off unresolved AI conversations. **Primitives:** private notes, calendar clocks with pause/resume meaning, routing, AI proposals, customer identity and notices. **Recovery:** wrong classifications are corrected with history; summaries never replace source; lost assignees return to a permitted queue; timer replay cannot create repeated escalations. Customer-visible responses and staff notes remain separate. **Slice:** Zendesk first-response SLA plus Intercom assignment/handover, excluding omnichannel telephony and autonomous support parity. [Zendesk SLA](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5600997516058-About-SLA-policies-and-how-they-work), [Intercom assignment](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/11961281-manage-and-troubleshoot-assignment-workflows).

### 10. CanIntake — reviewed document packets (new; compose with existing owners)

**Outcome:** a consultancy turns client questionnaires and attachments into complete case packets. Invite a scoped external submitter, collect missing items incrementally, extract candidate answers, cross-check conflicting documents and send precise clarification requests. Freeze accepted evidence and promote it through CanGrant/CanContract/CanPurchase operations as appropriate. **Primitives:** external invitations, file quarantine/finalization, versioned checklists, AI extraction, deadlines and explicit actor-specific views. **Recovery:** partial upload survives; extraction waits for finalized attachments; superseding files invalidate affected review; untrusted content cannot redirect invitation links. **Slice:** Airtable attachment/AI automation intake, not an unrestricted form builder. [Attachment processing failures](https://support.airtable.com/articles/6756755850-troubleshooting-airtable-automations) show why arrival and readiness must differ.

### 11. CanVendorReview — supplier onboarding (new small app; compose Purchase/Contract)

**Outcome:** procurement staff activate suppliers only after the company’s required finance/security/legal reviews. Parallel reviewers examine scoped evidence, request changes and approve frozen revisions with expiry; canonical supplier activation requires the completed policy. **Primitives:** multi-authority staged approval, confidential documents, effective dates, provider verification evidence and shared supplier identity. **Recovery:** an expired certificate reopens only the affected review; a lost reviewer is reassigned with history; banking changes require fresh verification; failing one review leaves the old approved supplier usable where policy permits. **Slice:** Zip supplier onboarding/intake-to-procure, not enterprise spend management. [Zip product workflow](https://zip.com/products/intake-to-procure).

### 12. CanSync — one concrete cross-system synchronization (new; first priority)

**Outcome:** an agency synchronizes approved CRM customer updates to its billing provider and shows unresolved differences. Declare authoritative fields, identity mapping and version checkpoints; process webhook/poll batches; detect conflicts; preview and authorize correction. **Primitives:** typed contracts, dedupe, cursors, bounded continuation, external outcomes and per-field ownership. **Recovery:** retries do not repeat completed writes; unknown outcomes reconcile; schema incompatibility stops affected work visibly; deleted identities are not silently recreated. **Slice:** a specific Zapier/Make integration with run history and recovery, not a general automation canvas or connector marketplace. [Zapier replay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/19220226086797-What-is-replay), [Make recovery](https://help.make.com/manage-incomplete-executions).

### 13. CanResearchBrief — monitored evidence to reviewed digest (new; compose Discover/Knowledge)

**Outcome:** a strategy team receives a short competitor briefing with attributable claims. Cluster collected evidence by story, compare changes to earlier issues, produce a cited draft, let an editor revise and freeze publication, then deliver once to authorized recipients. **Primitives:** story identity, evidence retention, AI synthesis, editorial versions, locales and scheduled delivery. **Recovery:** a withdrawn claim becomes a correction; an inaccessible source is labeled; partial coverage survives visibly; repeated delivery cannot silently regenerate different wording. **Slice:** Feedly automated newsletters/AlphaSense alert consumption, not paid research content replication. [Feedly newsletter dedupe](https://docs.feedly.com/article/847-article-deduplication-automated-newsletters).

### 14. Meeting-to-actions pack (enrich CanBoard)

**Outcome:** a consulting team turns meeting evidence into reviewed minutes and assigned actions. Optionally ingest externally transcribed audio, propose decisions/actions with evidence spans, ask the secretary to correct and finalize, then invoke canonical task owners. **Primitives:** transcript/file provenance, uncertain extraction, speaker attribution, frozen approval and bounded fanout. **Recovery:** ambiguous speech stays unresolved; recording consent and attendee access are explicit; no AI-inferred vote/quorum; changed minutes do not silently rewrite accepted task obligations. **Slice:** a Dify extraction/review workflow over meeting inputs, not conferencing or realtime transcription infrastructure. [Dify human review](https://dify.ai/blog/the-human-input-node-bringing-human-judgment-into-automated-workflows).

### 15. CanWorkbench — approved AI business tool runs (new)

**Outcome:** authorized staff run a named bounded task, such as preparing a refund recommendation, with a visible evidence/approval trail. Retrieve permitted records, call specified read tools, propose an exact operation, collect reviewer edits/approval and invoke it under current authority. **Primitives:** typed AI/tool results, execution budget, durable suspension, argument-version binding, cancelation and canonical operations. **Recovery:** denied calls return a useful outcome; approval expiry or changed records requires re-review; tools cannot escalate the initiator’s authority; model retries cannot repeat side effects. **Slice:** n8n human-reviewed AI tool calls/Retool agent approval, not a visual agent IDE. [n8n official documentation](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n-docs/blob/main/docs/build/integrate-ai/ai-examples/human-in-the-loop-for-tools.md), [Retool agent quickstart](https://docs.retool.com/agents/quickstart).

### 16. CanCreative — image generation with company review (new; compose Gallery)

**Outcome:** a marketing team commissions a bounded set of campaign image variants, reviews them and publishes approved originals with generation provenance. Freeze brief/reference permissions, budget and model settings; run jobs; associate results; record reviewer rejection/approval and license assumptions. **Primitives:** external media jobs, file provenance, model budgets, async cancelation, review and derived asset ownership. **Recovery:** partial batches are usable but explicit; unknown charged jobs reconcile before resubmission; rejected outputs remain restricted; a reference image grant does not authorize public publication. **Slice:** Open WebUI image generation plus Cloudinary review, not a full design suite. [Open WebUI features](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/), [Cloudinary moderation](https://cloudinary.com/documentation/cloudinary_moderation).

## Eight highest-information draft exercises

Start with **CanInbox, CanDiscover, CanChat, CanGallery, document-to-purchase, CanEnrich, CanSync and CanWorkbench**. These cover respectively private/shared message authority; recurring bounded discovery/provenance; conversational state and canceled AI work; binary media lifecycle; uncertain extraction versus exact financial evidence; provider waterfalls and paid-result reuse; conflicting external authorities; and durable human approval of tools. Draft one complete success path plus low-confidence/rejection, revocation, duplicate/unknown outcome and recovery paths for each. Existing business owners stay authoritative; composition should add no duplicated contact/supplier/payment model or app wrappers. Chat and gallery should be useful without AI; discovery should retain manual evidence review if extraction fails.

A useful initial composition is **SalesResearch = Discover + Enrich + CRM + Inbox**, with outreach optional. **ServiceOps = Inbox + Desk + Knowledge + Chat** shares support/customer authority. **Procurement = Intake + VendorReview + Purchase + Contract** shares suppliers. **CreativeOps = Gallery + Creative** shares one asset owner. These are candidate authored compositions, not required platform layers or fixed deployment decisions.

## Fair economics and stewardship

For each shortlisted slice, pin users, monthly messages/documents/assets, scheduled checks, provider calls, review minutes, turnaround target, retention and required source access. Compare a vendor configuration that achieves that outcome against generated Can authoring/repair, hosting/storage, model/search/OCR/data credits, provider licenses, review, maintenance, credentials, backups and incident handling. Include acquisition/import and exit work. Marginal cancellation savings can be zero when a company still needs the suite’s other capabilities; provider data and licensed research do not become free through Can. Do not invent cost totals before obtaining workload and plan evidence.

The potential Can advantage is company-specific ownership and several focused tools sharing one business model, with cheaper ordinary changes. Incumbents supply maintained connectors, operational recovery, collaboration conventions and support. Can may reduce seat/workflow subscriptions but transfers reliability, security updates and integration drift to its steward; open-source self-hosting has similar stewardship costs. Count continuing human review rather than treating AI output as labor-free. Classifier accuracy, dedupe quality and grounded-answer quality require evaluated examples and company acceptance evidence, not product marketing or compact source.

## Boundaries and unresolved design evidence

Out of scope: realtime collaborative canvas, video editor, complete email server, telephony/contact-center suite, generic Zapier/Retool/Dify clone, proprietary lead/research data replacement, unrestricted autonomous agent, legal/accounting certification and model-training platform. Optional OCR, search, indexing, email and media providers remain explicit proposed contracts until specified; this memo does not assert that current Can syntax can express every requirement.

The next design work should settle source-scoped AI retrieval, structured uncertain results with evidence, bounded model/tool budgets, cancelation/unknown outcomes, approval over exact proposed arguments, media originals/derivatives and resumable source progress. Current semantics may change; retain canonical operations, actual app identity and shared defaults where useful. Produce bounded `.md`/`.can` drafts only after selection, exposing gaps and inline behavior examples. No compiler/stdlib/runtime work is required to learn from these cases.
