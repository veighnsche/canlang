## Decision: extend the zero-dep client (no vscode-languageclient)

Adopting `vscode-languageclient` would need `node_modules` +
`@types/vscode`/`@types/node` and break the lane-01 gate, which pins
`--typeRoots /tmp/empty-typeroot`. Extending the hand-rolled client with
7 `register*Provider` calls + wider ambient shims was smaller and keeps
`tsc --strict` green under the existing gate.
