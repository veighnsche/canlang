## Four completion dimensions and limits

- Path catalogue and disposition: complete for 3,741 parent paths and 951 child input paths, plus newly created audit docs accounted in the desired tree.
- Substantive responsibilities/workflows: partial for the entire product. Complete traces above and in primary reviews do not certify every large checker/provider/migration/UI branch, every 2,017 document body, every fixture expectation, or each draft business journey. Unreviewed duties retain their owner and block dependent correctness/retirement claims.
- Independent challenge: completed for selected ownership/extraction/retention decisions and the corrected stale findings; not universal independent review of all retained behavior.
- Exact allocation: complete identity retention plus the explicitly selected successor leaves. Speculative broad splits/ports/legacy-codec and business-policy corrections remain gated; no undefined destination is executable.

The deliverable reaches the task/lane phase with these honest limits. Read-only path/metadata/source/AST inspection and artifact checks are new audit evidence; previous runtime results remain prior evidence only. This is not security certification, a successful native deployment, or achievement of the overall zero-diagnostic goal.
