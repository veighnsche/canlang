Canonical source-unit tables in those reviews are the governing allocation; the path inventory is the exhaustive identity-retention fallback, not a second conflicting symbol map. Any overlapping helper/visibility or unreviewed declaration closure blocks only that extraction until its defining owner resolves it.

No unit-test relocation or source-policy migration remains pending. Remaining checker, legacy profile, provider, migration and installed-compatibility duties are source-review or named C/V tasks, not another automatic file-splitting campaign.
