# CanRent requirements

Inherits [canlang requirements](../REQUIREMENTS.md) and [workspace operator context](WORKSPACE_OPERATOR.md), with [portfolio composition](PORTFOLIO.md). Companion draft: [CanRent.can](CanRent.can).

## Purpose and Adoption Goal

Be the workspace operator's authoritative booking app for coworking desks, day offices, meeting rooms, and rentable event spaces. Adoption depends on customers finding suitable availability, seeing the full price, and receiving a real reservation that reception can honor.

## Users and Permissions

Workspace managers configure resources, prices, availability, and cancellation policies for their granted locations. Reception creates bookings, checks customers in/out, and records no-shows. Verified customers book and manage their own reservations without seeing other customers' details. Only authorized finance staff record payment exceptions or refunds; customer accounts are not operator team members.

## Data and Ownership

Location stores name/address, timezone, currency, published opening/access hours, holidays, and arrival instructions. A resource is one identifiable desk, office, meeting room, or event space with type, capacity, amenities, accessible-facility information, hourly or daily rate, minimum duration/increment, setup buffer, and availability state. Each booking reserves one individually named resource or one configured pooled zone with a positive seat quantity; multi-resource carts remain deferred. Bookings retain customer/billing contact, attendee count, source, chosen intervals, price/tax/discount and cancellation-policy snapshots, confirmed hold identity/expiry, payment or on-account evidence, and scheduled/actual arrival/departure. Changes and cancellations remain attributed. Moving or retiring a resource with existing reservations requires explicit resolution of those reservations; editing its location cannot silently move customers to another site.

Add an explicitly configured pooled coworking zone as an alternative to an individually named resource. It stores available seat count, applicable dates/access hours, price unit, and capacity-history revisions. A pooled reservation records requested seats and a dated assignment if a specific desk is later allocated; do not count both the pool booking and its desk assignment as new capacity. Catalog locations remain the canonical source for other apps.

## Workflows and Business Rules

Customers search by location, date/time, resource type, party size, and amenities; only published eligible resources appear. Hour-priced reservations use non-empty half-open instant intervals. Day-priced reservations use local dates [start date, end date), at least one day, and explicit access hours expanded into held intervals using the calendar policy shown before confirmation. Availability checks include opening hours, party capacity, buffers, other holds/bookings, physical occupancy, and maintenance blocks. One resource authority checks and reserves all applicable intervals together. Snapshot price and terms before confirmation; later configuration changes cannot reprice or move a booking. An accepted quote handoff uses its frozen price/terms or returns a visible mismatch for authorized resolution; it never silently substitutes the current rate.

An unpaid checkout reserves capacity for a configurable hold window, initially 15 minutes. Confirm a paid booking only after authenticated payment evidence and reservation fulfillment; show pending and expired holds separately. A free booking or authorized on-account customer booking requires its own explicit fulfillment operation and recorded reason, rather than a fabricated provider payment. Staff-created walk-ins obey the same rules. On-account confirmation requires a customer account explicitly approved by finance for invoicing; reception cannot confer that approval. Hold expiry and settlement race at the reservation authority. Late successful payment tries to regain capacity; if unavailable, preserve received funds in a visible refund/alternative queue.

Cancellation applies the snapshotted cutoff/fee/refund policy, stops future access and obsolete reminders, and releases only the applicable reservation; refund status remains distinct. Record no-shows explicitly under the policy. Moving or extending a booking preserves the original reservation until the new availability decision succeeds and records any separately priced adjustment. Check-in requires an eligible confirmed booking in its permitted access window; a staff cancellation of occupied work removes future privileges but does not mark the resource physically clear; check-out records departure. A resource stays physically unavailable until reception clears occupancy, even if its planned end has passed. A buffer can delay resale after departure. Overtime and damage charges require separate authorized adjustments, never an unannounced rewrite of the original price.

A manager may request timed or indefinite maintenance closure through this authority. Reject new incompatible reservations and identify existing affected bookings for staff resolution without silently cancelling them. A room-backed tour or event uses this same authority; another app cannot keep its own competing room calendar.

A pooled-zone authority reserves bounded seat capacity over every requested interval; concurrent last-seat requests yield only affordable capacity. Capacity reductions or maintenance blocks preserve existing reservations and produce affected-booking conflicts for manager resolution. Pooled capacity, room capacity, attendee capacity and physical presence are separate measures. An overdue pooled occupant keeps only that booking's unresolved seat quantity physically occupied, not the whole zone; reception clears it explicitly and affected subsequent allocations remain visible for resolution.

Where a paid plan includes workspace units, request an identified allowance reservation from CanMember and a capacity hold from the applicable CanRent authority. Confirm only after both and any full monetary overage are fulfilled. Separate authorities retain recoverable pending coordination: if one hold fails, release the other provisional allocation once. A provider timeout leaves funds/allowance reservations pending until reconciled, not spendable again. A cancellation restores only the snapshotted refundable allowance under its original period and releases the applicable capacity; an expired period cannot receive usable new credits. Late settlement after expiry re-checks both allowance and space, or enters refund/alternative review. Quotes display included units, money overage, and the policy before confirmation.

## Background Actions

Expire unpaid holds; reconcile uncertain payment/refund and authority-to-view outcomes. Send a confirmation with location, booked resource, dates/access hours, arrival instructions, terms, and own-booking link after fulfillment. Schedule a revision-bound pre-arrival reminder and notify the customer of cancellation or confirmed material changes. Flag overdue occupancy and affected future bookings for reception; never auto-clear an occupied room because a timer fired.

## Error Handling

Explain sold-out resources, insufficient party capacity, invalid hours/duration, maintenance closure, ineligible membership, and failed moves without losing the existing booking. Repeated requests/events cannot produce duplicate reservations, charges, admissions, or releases. A payment timeout remains uncertain, and a received payment without available space remains visible for resolution. A stale reception screen cannot clear another booking's occupancy. Show delivery and synchronization failures rather than claiming checkout, cancellation, or repair release has succeeded everywhere.

## Scope and Completion

A customer can find and pay for a day office or hourly meeting room, retrieve the price/terms and arrival instructions, and cancel under the recorded policy. Reception can handle a walk-in, no-show, late departure, and maintenance closure. Competing requests cannot sell the same resource; late payment and unavailable quote handoff have recoverable visible outcomes. Rates remain frozen and rescheduling updates holds/reminders. The fuller operator scope includes individually bookable resources and pooled coworking zones, one resource/zone and currency per booking, full remaining monetary-balance payment or authorized on-account confirmation, and explicitly reserved membership allowances. Bundled multi-resource orders, deposits, partial monetary payments, automatic door provisioning, and long-term lease administration remain deferred.

Additionally complete when two members cannot take the last pooled seat or shared allowance, failure of one resource/allowance hold releases its counterpart once, and full-money overage is confirmed before admission. Pooled coworking and plan usage allowances are required in the fuller operator profile; deposits, partial monetary payments and multi-resource carts remain deferred.

Frontend acceptance journeys:

- Two customers request the last pooled seat: one succeeds, the other receives current availability without duplicate capacity/allowance consumption. Failed counterpart holds release once; money overage remains visible until fulfilled.
- Reception sees an overdue occupied room despite its planned end; explicit checkout clears occupancy. A failed reschedule preserves the original booking, and late payment without capacity enters refund/alternative review rather than admission.
