# CanRent requirements

Inherits [canlang requirements](../REQUIREMENTS.md) and [workspace operator context](WORKSPACE_OPERATOR.md), with [portfolio composition](PORTFOLIO.md). Companion draft: [CanRent.can](CanRent.can).

## Purpose and Adoption Goal

Be the workspace operator's authoritative booking app for coworking desks, day offices, meeting rooms, and rentable event spaces. Adoption depends on customers finding suitable availability, seeing the full price, and receiving a real reservation that reception can honor.

## Users and Permissions

Workspace managers configure resources, prices, availability, and cancellation policies for their granted locations. Reception creates bookings, checks customers in/out, and records no-shows. Verified customers book and manage their own reservations without seeing other customers' details. Only authorized finance staff record payment exceptions or refunds; customer accounts are not operator team members.

## Data and Ownership

Location stores name/address, timezone, currency, published opening/access hours, holidays, and arrival instructions. A resource is one identifiable desk, office, meeting room, or event space with type, capacity, amenities, accessible-facility information, hourly or daily rate, minimum duration/increment, setup buffer, and availability state. Each booking reserves one individually named resource or one configured pooled zone with a positive seat quantity; multi-resource carts remain deferred. Bookings retain customer/billing contact, attendee count, source, chosen intervals, price/tax/discount and cancellation-policy snapshots, confirmed hold identity/expiry, payment or on-account evidence, and scheduled/actual arrival/departure. Changes and cancellations remain attributed. Moving or retiring a resource with existing reservations requires explicit resolution of those reservations; editing its location cannot silently move customers to another site.

Add an explicitly configured pooled coworking zone as an alternative to an individually named resource. It stores available seat count, applicable dates/access hours, price unit, and capacity-history revisions. A pooled reservation records requested seats and a dated assignment if a specific desk is later allocated; do not count both the pool booking and its desk assignment as new capacity. Catalog locations remain the canonical source for other apps.

## Workflows and Business Rules

Customers search by location, date/time, resource type, party size, and amenities; only published eligible resources appear. Hour-priced reservations use non-empty half-open instant intervals. Day-priced reservations use local dates [start date, end date), at least one day, and explicit access hours expanded into held intervals using the calendar policy shown before confirmation. Availability checks include opening hours, party capacity, buffers, other holds/bookings, physical occupancy, and maintenance blocks. One resource authority checks and reserves all applicable intervals together. Snapshot price and terms before confirmation; later configuration changes cannot reprice or move a booking. An accepted quote handoff uses its frozen price/terms or returns a visible mismatch for authorized resolution; it never silently substitutes the current rate.

An unpaid checkout reserves capacity for a configurable hold window, initially 15 minutes. Confirm a paid booking only after authenticated payment evidence and reservation fulfillment; show pending and expired holds separately. A free booking or authorized on-account customer booking requires its own explicit fulfillment operation and recorded reason, rather than a fabricated provider payment. Staff-created walk-ins obey the same rules. On-account confirmation requires a customer account explicitly approved by finance for invoicing; reception cannot confer that approval. Hold expiry and settlement race at the reservation authority. Late successful payment tries to regain capacity; if unavailable, preserve received funds in a visible refund/alternative queue.

Cancellation applies the snapshotted cutoff/fee/refund policy, stops future access and obsolete reminders, and releases only the applicable reservation; refund status remains distinct. Record no-shows explicitly under the policy. Moving or extending a booking preserves the original reservation until the new availability decision succeeds and records any separately priced adjustment. Check-in requires an eligible confirmed booking in its permitted access window; a staff cancellation of occupied work removes future privileges but does not mark the resource physically clear; check-out records departure. A resource stays physically unavailable until reception clears occupancy, even if its planned end has passed. A buffer can delay resale after departure. Overtime and damage charges require separate authorized adjustments, never an unannounced rewrite of the original price.

A manager may request timed or indefinite maintenance closure through this authority. Reject new incompatible reservations and identify existing affected bookings for staff resolution without silently cancelling them. A room-backed tour or event uses this same authority; another app cannot keep its own competing room calendar.

A pooled-zone authority reserves bounded seat capacity over every requested interval; concurrent last-seat requests yield only affordable capacity. Capacity reductions or maintenance blocks preserve existing reservations and produce affected-booking conflicts for manager resolution. Pooled capacity, room capacity, attendee capacity and physical presence are separate measures. An overdue pooled occupant keeps only that booking's unresolved seat quantity physically occupied, not the whole zone; reception clears it explicitly and affected subsequent allocations remain visible for resolution.

Where a paid plan includes workspace units, request an identified allowance reservation from CanMember and a capacity hold from the applicable CanRent authority. Confirm only after both and any full monetary overage are fulfilled. Separate authorities retain recoverable pending coordination: if one hold fails, release the other provisional allocation once. A provider timeout leaves funds/allowance reservations pending until reconciled, not spendable again. A cancellation restores only the snapshotted refundable allowance under its original period and releases the applicable capacity; an expired period cannot receive usable new credits. Late settlement after expiry re-checks both allowance and space, or enters refund/alternative review. Quotes display included units, money overage, and the policy before confirmation.

## Admin and Management Surfaces

End-user admin is required for published workspace catalog and booking-policy administration. The operator must control the prices, capacities, hours and booking policies offered to all customers. A customer or reception booker cannot grant themselves cheaper prices, extra capacity or broader access.

Provide a protected shared catalog/pricing/booking-policy view for authorized workspace managers. Reservations, walk-ins, arrival/departure, downtime decisions and customer service remain operational views, not extra admin areas. Changes retain committed snapshots and affected-booking rules; no developer/service configuration is exposed. See [end-user administration scope](ADMIN_SURFACES.md).

## Interfaces and Integrations

Use D1 for records and views, one Durable Object reservation authority per resource, the Stripe payment adapter for collection/refund reconciliation, and EmailService for booking messages. Declare workspace hold/confirm/move/release, downtime/affected-booking lookup, and own-booking/eligibility capabilities. Billing may use a configured CanInvoice charge/settlement contract; otherwise the payment adapter owns collection. Configure exactly one collection owner per charge. Preserve recoverable pending work when provider, authority, or mirror updates cannot commit together. Member eligibility, quote handoffs, event venues, and repair references use explicit scoped contracts. Logical check-in records do not operate door hardware.

In the composed operator profile CanInvoice owns the monetary charge for a booking, while CanMember owns allowance hold/consume/release. Neither a credit hold nor an issued invoice alone confirms a booking. Use CanCustomer for organization-role checks and CanReport for reproducible portfolio measures. Customer/employee removal rechecks prospective actions and reports affected confirmed bookings for explicit resolution.

## Background Actions

Expire unpaid holds; reconcile uncertain payment/refund and authority-to-view outcomes. Send a confirmation with location, booked resource, dates/access hours, arrival instructions, terms, and own-booking link after fulfillment. Schedule a revision-bound pre-arrival reminder and notify the customer of cancellation or confirmed material changes. Flag overdue occupancy and affected future bookings for reception; never auto-clear an occupied room because a timer fired.

## Error Handling

Explain sold-out resources, insufficient party capacity, invalid hours/duration, maintenance closure, ineligible membership, and failed moves without losing the existing booking. Repeated requests/events cannot produce duplicate reservations, charges, admissions, or releases. A payment timeout remains uncertain, and a received payment without available space remains visible for resolution. A stale reception screen cannot clear another booking's occupancy. Show delivery and synchronization failures rather than claiming checkout, cancellation, or repair release has succeeded everywhere.

## Scope and Completion

A customer can find and pay for a day office or hourly meeting room, retrieve the price/terms and arrival instructions, and cancel under the recorded policy. Reception can handle a walk-in, no-show, late departure, and maintenance closure. Competing requests cannot sell the same resource; late payment and unavailable quote handoff have recoverable visible outcomes. Rates remain frozen and rescheduling updates holds/reminders. The fuller operator scope includes individually bookable resources and pooled coworking zones, one resource/zone and currency per booking, full remaining monetary-balance payment or authorized on-account confirmation, and explicitly reserved membership allowances. Bundled multi-resource orders, deposits, partial monetary payments, automatic door provisioning, and long-term lease administration remain deferred.

Additionally complete when two members cannot take the last pooled seat or shared allowance, failure of one resource/allowance hold releases its counterpart once, and full-money overage is confirmed before admission. Pooled coworking and plan usage allowances are required in the fuller operator profile; deposits, partial monetary payments and multi-resource carts remain deferred.

Frontend acceptance journeys:

- Two customers request the last pooled seat: one succeeds, the other receives current availability without duplicate capacity/allowance consumption. Failed counterpart holds release once; money overage remains visible until fulfilled.
- Reception sees an overdue occupied room despite its planned end; explicit checkout clears occupancy. A failed reschedule preserves the original booking, and late payment without capacity enters refund/alternative review rather than admission.

## Composition and Ownership

Recommended placement: Workspace. Own catalog/pricing, reservations/availability, fulfillment/occupancy, and local reporting in separate packages. Compose with CanMember; use CanInvoice for collection in the composed operator profile. CanReport owns cross-app metric definitions. See [portfolio composition and ownership](PORTFOLIO.md). Focused example requirements remain valid; composing packages does not require a new source file or an independent deployment per package.

## Authored draft workflows and contracts

CanRent.can and its hand-written proposed JavaScript target now author the catalog, hourly/day availability and holds, positive party/capacity quantities, pool desk assignment, capacity revisions/conflicts, accepted quote handoff, frozen commercial policy, paid/free/approved-account fulfillment, no-shows, actual occupancy, moves, extensions, separate finance adjustments, notices and payment/allowance reconciliation. The target imports remain proposed contracts; no compiler, runtime, standard library, UI library, adapter or runner is implemented by this work.

Location owns structured WeeklyHours, ClosedDate and DateHours and exports is_open; its display hours text is never parsed as authority. Resource windows and day calendars narrow those hours. Day holds atomically reserve each published access interval and its frozen setup/departure buffers; nights between service days remain available. Frozen QuoteDocument intervals are validated for exact bounds and nonoverlap, and day quotes must match every published resource day. Accepted quote lines, tax, discount, issuer, terms and cancellation deadline reach the invoice without current-rate substitution. Resource capacity units, attendee limits and commercial quantities remain separate.

Reception reads a limited operational Booking projection and creates a reservation through the existing hold/prepare operations. Selecting another account requires current reception location scope plus that account's canonical customer ownership or company booker/administrator grant. The operation actor remains reception. Finance approval is owned by CanCustomer and records approved locations; Rent records the actual approval row/version and reason. Admission rechecks active customer, current approval, full money coverage or explicit free fulfillment, consumed allowance and capacity inside the actual service window. Physical possession persists through cancellation and overdue timers until explicit checkout, including the frozen departure buffer.

Billing settlement is cumulative and ordered by the invoice owner's ledger revision. Original invoice amount, collected/refunded money and uncertain attempts remain distinct from booking price and current service balance. An issued invoice or successful send never means paid. Matching owner snapshots and correlated completed outcomes drive confirmation and refund evidence; null reconciliation never proves cancellation. Terminal bookings retain their status after late failed callbacks. Received money that cannot be fulfilled remains in the authorized finance review queue.

Membership reserve carries exact intervals, unit, duration, rate, customer, account and resource location. Rent freezes those inputs before request and verifies the owner's typed overage outcome. Allowance is reserved before charging only the real monetary overage; included units use explicit free fulfillment. Reserve/consume/release and reconciliation retain pending work and same-source fences. Consumed allowances restore only under the recorded refundable policy and original owner period. A member move retains the original capacity and consumed allocation while staging a candidate, separately charges a larger balance, or records an actual refundable credit. Commit adopts the new intervals only after fresh capacity and owner consumption. Cancellation first cleans the candidate, then restores the predecessor; uncertain cleanup can be reconciled and retried with the same captured restoration flag. Credit refunds select actual original/adjustment charges and become paid only from cumulative refunded evidence.

Appointment venue reservations are noncommercial siblings of Booking at the same resource authority. They share capacity without fictitious free or paid evidence. Staging retains the predecessor and deduplicates only its overlap. Provisional hold/stage outcomes are pending; confirmation is separately committed. Release-before-arrival creates a resource-scoped fence; absent and already-released rows still emit authoritative release outcomes. Quote hold/release outcomes carry a separate kind and owner version, so a late provisional hold cannot confirm an accepted booking.

## Commercial sales attribution

Hour and day holds accept one optional canonical `sales_attribution.Capture`. The submitting account must be verified, equal the booking account and captured account, and use the latest eligible capture for that customer, billing location and actual resource kind (`desk`, `office`, `meeting` or `event_space`). The booking freezes the capture's code, exclusive program, original capture time and window. Referral and partner origins use that same contract; no extra referral/affiliate checkout inputs are introduced. A receptionist creating a booking for another account cannot attach the receptionist's own capture. Later prepare, move and extension operations cannot replace the origin.

An accepted quote carries the same four frozen origin values from Propose's user acceptance, along with its explicit product identity. Rent requires a complete valid attribution bundle or none, checks the purchase timestamp against the original window, and rejects an attributed product that differs from the owning resource kind. It does not select a new capture during asynchronous fulfillment. Quote and internal venue bookings may remain unattributed. Ordinary internal venue holds are reservation coordination rather than standalone monetary sales.

The committed `rent_reservations.CommercialSaleChanged {value:invoice.SaleMilestone}` source event maps to `invoice.SalesIngressV1.observed` through a verified deployment mapping restricted to this producer's team and billing-source namespace. Its `source` is the actual original `Booking.source`; its amount is the immutable `monetary_due` recorded when the original charge is queued, including the membership allowance's actual monetary overage after frozen discount and tax. Quote charges now record that same original amount explicitly. Current service balances, movement charges, overtime and damage adjustments never substitute for or enlarge the original sale. Product and billing location are frozen from the real resource; purchase time is the original hour/day hold creation or accepted quote's `accepted_at`, independent of later service completion. Legacy rows without those transaction snapshots are excluded rather than backfilled with guesses.

`paid` requires fulfilled space, consumed or unnecessary allowance, positive original money, actual cumulative collection/refund evidence and no cancellation/refund work. `completed` additionally requires recorded physical checkout and carries its actual departure timestamp. Cancellation, no-show, cancellation fences and refunds permanently reverse the source; review/expiry or lost financial/allowance fulfillment reverse a previously published sale. Pre-sale expiry retains the existing late-payment recovery rules. The refund cutoff does not prohibit cancellation, so Rent emits no `cancellation_passed` claim. Free/included bookings and unpaid on-account bookings are not asserted as paid commercial money.

One unique `CommercialSale` child per Booking stores phase and monotonically increasing source revision; identical phases emit nothing and reversal cannot revive. Its updates leave `Booking.version` unchanged under DESIGN §2, preserving correspondence and revision-bound arrival reminders. `ReservationChanged` forwards to the sales check; additional failed-charge, invalidated allowance/consumption and failed replacement branches trigger only that check rather than duplicate customer notices. Invoice independently joins the exact source to its actual invoice/payment/refund ledger and reviewed complete customer history before emitting `SalesV1.qualification`. Rent does not claim first-customer proof or qualify referrals/affiliates directly.
